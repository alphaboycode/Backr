SLIDE 1: THE EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Backr is a lean, transparent, all-or-nothing crowdfunding protocol deployed on 
the BOT Network. It gives creators a censorship-resistant way to raise capital 
in native BOT while ensuring backers have 100% guarantee of refunds if the 
campaign does not achieve its target goal before the deadline.

Key Highlights:
- 0% Platform Fees: 100% of capital raised flows to creators.
- 100% Onchain: No IPFS reliance, no proprietary backend, no database downtime.
- Guaranteed Refunds: Automated pull-style refunds for failed campaigns.
- Overfunding Supported: Creators receive total raised, not capped at goal.
- Battle-Tested: Built with Solidity ^0.8.24, OpenZeppelin 5.x, and verified 
  via stateful fuzzing invariant tests.

SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM WITH LEGACY CROWDFUNDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Traditional Web2 platforms (Kickstarter, GoFundMe, Indiegogo) and centralized 
funding solutions suffer from deep structural friction:

1. Predatory Platform Fees:
   - Platforms charge 5% to 8% in platform fees PLUS 3% to 5% in payment 
     processing cuts, siphoning up to 12% of total capital away from builders.

2. Custodial Risk & Arbitrary Freezes:
   - Central intermediaries hold funds in opaque bank accounts. Accounts can 
     be arbitrarily frozen, delayed, or cancelled due to banking restrictions 
     or subjective terms of service enforcement.

3. Geographic & Financial Exclusion:
   - Over 1.4 billion unbanked or underbanked individuals cannot participate 
     in global Kickstarter campaigns due to credit card and cross-border limits.

4. Slow & Unreliable Payouts:
   - Creators often wait 14 to 30 business days after a campaign ends before 
     funds clear traditional merchant accounts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3: THE SOLUTION - BACKR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Backr replaces centralized escrow companies with a single, audited, autonomous 
smart contract:

1. Trustless All-or-Nothing Mechanism:
   - If (totalRaised >= goal) after deadline: Creator claims 100% of funds.
   - If (totalRaised < goal) after deadline: Backers claim 100% refund.
   - Zero subjective human discretion.

2. 0% Fee Architecture:
   - No platform cut, no protocol tax, no hidden extraction. Only minimal gas 
     on the ultra-fast BOT Network.

3. Permissionless & Global:
   - Anyone with a Web3 wallet can launch a campaign or back an idea in seconds. 
     No KYC friction, no country blacklists, no banking delays.

4. Immediate Settlement:
   - Payouts and refunds are executable the second a campaign deadline passes.

SLIDE 4: HOW IT WORKS (USER JOURNEY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A streamlined 3-step cycle:

STEP 1: CREATE
- Creator connects wallet and defines:
  * Title (1 to 100 chars)
  * Description (1 to 1000 chars)
  * Target funding goal in BOT
  * Duration in days (1 to 90 days)
- Stored directly onchain via an immutable struct with an auto-incrementing ID.

STEP 2: PLEDGE
- Backers pledge native BOT tokens to the active campaign.
- Contract tracks individual pledges and aggregate totalRaised.
- Backers can make multiple pledges that accumulate seamlessly.
- Overfunding is encouraged: backers can keep pledging until deadline.

STEP 3: RESOLUTION & PULL-SETTLEMENT
- Case A (Success): Deadline arrives and totalRaised >= goal.
  Creator calls withdraw() -> Smart contract sends totalRaised directly to creator.
- Case B (Failure): Deadline arrives and totalRaised < goal.
  Backers call refund() -> Smart contract returns exact pledge to each backer.

SLIDE 5: ARCHITECTURE & SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Security is paramount in crowdfunding escrow. Backr follows industry best 
practices:

1. Checks-Effects-Interactions (CEI):
   - Internal accounting (withdrawn flag or zeroing pledge balances) occurs 
     BEFORE external transfers.

2. OpenZeppelin 5.x ReentrancyGuard:
   - pledge(), withdraw(), and refund() are protected with nonReentrant guards.

3. Pull-Payment Architecture:
   - Backr never loops over backer arrays or pushes ETH/BOT to external accounts. 
   - Every withdrawal and refund is an isolated pull, completely eliminating 
     Denial-of-Service (DoS) and gas exhaustion vulnerabilities.

4. Zero Fallback / Receive:
   - Plain token transfers without call data revert automatically, preventing 
     accidental misdirected funds.

5. Rigorous Testing & Invariant Verification:
   - 15 comprehensive unit tests covering edge cases, reverts, and malicious actors.
   - Invariant fuzzing test executing 128,000 calls across 256 randomized runs 
     proving that contract balance ALWAYS equals total unwithdrawn, unrefunded pledges.
